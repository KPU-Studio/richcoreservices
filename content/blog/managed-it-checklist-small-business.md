# A Practical Managed IT Checklist for Small Businesses

Most small businesses don't need a big IT department — they need the important
things done consistently. Here's the short list we work through with every
client, and why each one matters.

## 1. Keep everything patched

Out-of-date software is the most common way small businesses get breached.
Operating systems, browsers, and business apps should update on a schedule, not
whenever someone remembers. Managed patching handles this automatically across
every device.

## 2. Back up — and test the restore

A backup you've never restored is a hope, not a plan. We back up business data
on a schedule and periodically confirm it can actually be recovered, so a failed
drive or a ransomware event is an inconvenience, not a closure.

## 3. Secure email and Microsoft 365

Email is where most attacks start. Multi-factor authentication, sensible sharing
defaults, and basic compliance configuration close the easy doors without
slowing your team down.

## 4. Have a real way to get help

When something breaks, "email a guy and wait" costs you the day. A responsive
help desk with a clear response window keeps small problems small.
